IEstats Privacy Policy

Effective October 20, 2026

IEstats is a stats and scouting app for VEX V5 Robotics Competition teams, coaches and parents. It doesn't collect, sell or share personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your settings (the teams you follow, nicknames, your role, theme and font), checklists, scorer and whiteboard work, and your own VEX API key if you add one (kept in the iOS Keychain) are stored only on your device. The app's widgets and Live Activity read them from your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

What the app downloads

IEstats downloads public competition results (teams, events, matches, rankings, skills and awards) from the VEX Events API at events.vex.com, and data files the developer publishes on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, these include the team or event you look up and your device's network address, and they're handled under the privacy policies of VEX and GitHub. IEstats doesn't send them anything else about you.

Children

IEstats is made for students, coaches and parents. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13.

TestFlight

If you test IEstats through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares the feedback, screenshots and crash reports you choose to send, and the email address you joined with, with the developer, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email the address on the IEstats support page, or use Send Beta Feedback in TestFlight.

IEstats isn't affiliated with or endorsed by VEX Robotics, the REC Foundation or the Global Robotics & Science Foundation.
